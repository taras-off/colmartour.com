## Who it's for

The audio guide is a good fit if you want to get to know Colmar's history without working around a group's meeting time or a tour guide's pace. You can start in the morning or later in the afternoon, linger at a house you like, skip what doesn't interest you, and come back to the recordings another day. It also works well for families with kids and for anyone who'd rather see the town without a tour group.

If you'd like to ask questions as you go or interact with a guide, a live guided tour is the better choice: the audio guide tells stories, but it can't answer questions.

## How it works

1. Buy the audio guide, and you'll get an email with activation instructions.
2. Install the TouringBee app on iOS or Android and activate your purchase.
3. Download the tour in advance, ideally over Wi-Fi. You only need an internet connection to buy, activate, and download; after that, the tour and map work offline.
4. Head to the starting point and follow the route on the map. The map shows where you are, and you start the recording at each stop yourself — nothing plays automatically.

There's no guide waiting at the start, and the tour isn't tied to a set time slot: you decide when to begin. You can stop partway through and pick up where you left off later — your access lasts a year.

## The route: where it goes and what you'll hear

The walk starts at Parc du Champ de Mars, runs through the historic center, and ends in Little Venice. You can complete the entire route on foot, with no transportation needed between stops. Once you finish, you can stay by the water or walk over to the covered market.

The route takes you through the historic center, home to the House of Heads, Pfister House, St. Martin's Church, and other well-known sights. It doesn't cover everything worth seeing in town, though.

You can easily find a list of Colmar's sights on your own. The audio guide offers something different: a ready-made route through the historic center, with stories about its past, its buildings, and its legends. You'll find out, for example, why Colmar once had its own currency and how the town is connected to Japanese animation. We'll save the rest for your walk.

## Before you go

Download the tour ahead of time so you don't have to rely on a signal, and make sure your phone is charged. Wear comfortable shoes: much of the old town is paved with cobblestones. If you plan to step into active churches, dress appropriately for a place of worship.

You can start at any time. If you'd like to stop by the market, a church, or a museum along the way, check their opening hours first. The walk also fits easily into [a day in Colmar](/one-day-in-colmar/): the old town in the morning, then a museum or a boat ride after lunch.
