Best Time to Visit Colmar: What the Town Is Like in Each Season

Easter markets, Tuesday folk evenings, the wine fair, autumn in the vineyards, Christmas. Colmar has no dead season — just a different town in different months.

By Eugene · Updated October 2026

Colmar audio guide — €9.99 Stays in Colmar

Colmar doesn't shut down for winter. It changes. In spring, Easter chalets appear in the centre; on summer Tuesdays, Alsatian folk groups perform on the square; in autumn the vineyards around town change colour; and in December the town revolves around its Christmas markets. Each season also has its own Alsatian specialities.

So the question isn't "when is best" but "which version of Colmar do you want". The old town is there all year round. Our audio guide works in any season: 27 stops, about an hour and a half, start whenever you like. Only the town around you changes.

Spring: Easter markets and a festive centre

Colmar greets spring with a celebration. Around Easter, two Easter markets open in the centre: colourful chalets with around 70 craftspeople from across the region, flowers throughout the historic centre and a mini-farm right in town. In 2027 they run from 19 March to 11 April.

Alsace and Moselle have their own calendar of public holidays, and Good Friday is on it. Bear that in mind if you're planning an Easter trip.

On the table: lamala, a sponge-cake Easter lamb dusted with icing sugar.

Summer: Tuesday music and the wine fair

Summer brings a packed calendar.

Folk evenings. From June to early September, on Tuesdays at 8pm, Alsatian folk groups perform on the Place de l'Ancienne Douane. Free entry.

International festival. In early July, Colmar's International Festival brings classical music to St Matthew's Church, the municipal theatre and the Koïfhus. The programme is published on festival-colmar.com.

Wine fair. In late July and early August, the Foire aux Vins d'Alsace is both a fair of local produce and a music festival. Concerts take place in the open-air theatre at the exhibition centre. Dates are on foire-colmar.com.

What else. From May to September, the Colmar-Houssen leisure lake north of town has a sandy beach and an inflatable water park. On summer Thursdays, Wettolsheim runs vineyard walks with a winemaker and a tasting; book through the tourist office.

Summer in Alsace means tarte flambée, pretzels and kougelhopf, sweet or savoury.

Mind the heat. July and August are Colmar's hottest months. Plan your old-town walk for the morning or the evening.

Autumn: wine season and choucroute

Colmar is known as the capital of Alsace wine, and autumn is when you feel it most. The vineyards around town change colour, and autumn brings a classic pairing: a glass of white Alsace wine and choucroute.

If you're coming for wine, autumn is the natural choice. Colmar itself makes a convenient starting point for the Wine Route. How to plan that day without driving after tastings is covered in our guide to the Wine Route.

Late autumn is a transition between the grape harvest and Christmas. Canal boats run all year, on a reduced timetable in winter. The tourist trains run for most of the year, but their schedules change in winter and over the Christmas season. In Turckheim, the night watchman makes his rounds from May to October and over the Christmas period.

Christmas: a season of its own

From late November to late December, Colmar hosts six Christmas markets, and it becomes a different town: lights, garlands, mulled wine, crowds. It's one of Colmar's main tourist seasons, so expect large numbers of visitors.

We cover the markets themselves in Colmar Christmas markets. For ideas on adding nearby villages, see Christmas markets near Colmar. Choosing between Colmar and Strasbourg? See Colmar or Strasbourg for Christmas.

Winter after the holidays

After Christmas, a visit is less about big seasonal events. The old town is still there, and the canal boats run all year.

At carnival time, Alsatians make schankalas, little almond doughnuts. For New Year, they bake a sweet brioche.

Bear in mind: January and February are the coldest months, with frequent overnight frosts.

What's the weather like in Colmar in each season?

Winters here are cold, with frosty nights. July and August are the warmest months of the year. Spring and early autumn are usually cooler.

In practice: in summer, plan your old-town walk for the morning or evening. For the Christmas markets, dress for winter and check the forecast before you go. For long walks without the summer heat, April–May and September–October work well.

Common mistakes

Forgetting about Good Friday. Alsace and Moselle have their own calendar of public holidays, so over the Easter weekend check opening hours for the places you want to visit in advance.

Planning a summer walk for midday. July and August are the hottest months. The old town is more pleasant in the morning or evening.

Going by last year's dates. Festivals and fairs come round at roughly the same time each year, but exact dates change. Check the events' official websites.

Thinking there's nothing to see after Christmas. The boats run all year, and the historic centre can be explored out of season too.

FAQ

What's the best month to visit Colmar?

It depends on what you want. For festivities, December for the markets or spring for Easter. For music, summer. For wine, autumn. For mild walking weather, April–May or September–October.

When is the wine fair?

Usually in late July and early August. Exact dates are on foire-colmar.com.

When are Colmar's Christmas markets?

From late November to late December. Details in Colmar Christmas markets.

Is Colmar worth visiting in winter after the holidays?

Yes, if you're coming for the town itself rather than for events. Allow for the cold and the short days.

What's the weather like in Colmar in summer?

July and August are the warmest months of the year. For long walks, mornings and evenings work best.
